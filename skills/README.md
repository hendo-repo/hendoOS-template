# Canonical skills

Only the three functionality-critical OS spine capabilities are canonical here:
`session-agent`, `closeout`, and `self-audit`. They are concise re-authorings of
the accepted behavior in the pinned upstream framework, not bulk copies of the
legacy shell system. Their provenance and MIT license are recorded in
`manifest.json` and `NOTICE`.

All other historical or operator skills remain outside hendoOS until a later
task-specific review demonstrates a current need. Harness realizations are
generated from these packages; never hand-edit a rendered copy.
